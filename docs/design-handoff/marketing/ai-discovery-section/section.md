## Layout Notes

We essentially have 3 Sections:
1. Header: The Header along with the description of the section with A CTA Button to run free AEO Audit
2. Graph: We essentially for now need to keep a placeholder graph which is rising which represents our growth in the metrics
3. Metrics: Metrics is the highlight of the website where it will situate our center highlight metric and the additional 4 metrics along with their descriptions will be in a grid format below the Center metric. 

Desktop Layout:
- The Layout will be horizontal.
- The Header and Graph will be in the left part taking 40%. 
- The Metrics will take up the rest of the space in the right.
- The Header will above the graph.
- The Center Metric in the metrics will above on the additional metrics

Tablet Layout:
- The Layout will be vertical.
- First the header, then the graph and then the Center Metric and the additional metrics.
- The Header will be on the left aligned 
- The Graph will be center aligned
- The Metrics will be right aligned

Mobile Layout:
- The mobile layout will be similar to the tablet layout but will be compressed for mobile view.
